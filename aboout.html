About this workspace

This page is less about roles and more about how I think and work.

I am an Electronics & Computer Engineering student with a strong interest in building intelligent systems that combine software, hardware, and data. I enjoy working on problems where the structure is not immediately obvious and needs to be discovered through exploration.

My work usually starts with understanding the system at a conceptual level, breaking it into components, experimenting with small parts, and iterating toward something functional and meaningful.

I value clarity, documentation, and curiosity more than rushing to outcomes.
