Water Supply Management System

A full-stack web application designed to automate and digitize municipal water supply operations. The system integrates an Oracle XE 10g database with a Flask backend and a React.js frontend.

Key Capabilities

  • Customer, connection, and meter management
  • Automated billing using PL/SQL triggers
  • Payment processing and validation
  • Admin and customer dashboards

Technology Stack

Oracle XE 10g · PL/SQL · Flask · React.js · cx_Oracle

The system ensures data consistency and automation through relational views, triggers, and RESTful APIs.
