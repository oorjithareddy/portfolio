Smart Diet Recommendation System

A personalized diet recommendation system designed to generate meal plans based on health conditions, dietary preferences, and nutritional needs.

Key Features

  • Health-aware food filtering
  • Vegetarian and non-vegetarian support
  • Meal planning for breakfast, lunch, and dinner
  • PDF report generation

Technology

Python · Tkinter · Pandas · NumPy · Rule-based logic

The system focuses on explainability and usability rather than black-box optimization.
